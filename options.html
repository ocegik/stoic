The Daily Standard

Two thresholds that decide whether a day counts as held — the basis for streaks, XP and achievements.

min/day

A day is only held if leisure-category time stays at or under this many minutes.

A day is only held if the focus score reaches this value — waived on a day with under 30 minutes tracked.
