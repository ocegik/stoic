Stoic

Sixty seconds before a site opens, and an honest record of where your attention actually went.

Nothing here is designed to make you feel bad about a number. It measures what happened and it stops what you asked it to stop.

Why it can see every site

To time a tab, Stoic has to read the address of the tab you are looking at. That is why Chrome asked for access to every site — there is no smaller permission that does it.

It keeps a local time ledger for every domain you visit, so it can tell you where the day went. None of it leaves this machine. There is no account, no server, and nothing to sign in to.

Start with one site

One rule is enough to begin. You can add more whenever a site earns one.
